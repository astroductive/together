How it works

Every ASL entry stores the recorded landmark sequence of a native signer — the same 543-point skeleton the recognition models are trained on — replayed as a smooth animated avatar you can watch at a comfortable pace, as many times as you need. Egyptian ArSL entries play the original reference video clips.

Search matches both the English word and its Arabic translation, and category filters (food, family, actions, and more) let you browse the vocabulary the way you'd study it. Each entry opens with one tap and can be replayed instantly.

Use cases

Learners

Look up a sign before using it in conversation, and replay the motion until the handshape feels natural.

Families

Classrooms

Teachers project an entry and let the whole class shadow the motion together.

Quick reference

Mid-conversation on the dashboard and unsure of a sign? The dictionary is one click away in the sidebar.

Built on the same data as the AI

The dictionary is not a separate content library — it renders the exact landmark sequences and video references the recognition and avatar models are built on. What you study here is precisely what the platform understands, so practice transfers one-to-one to live translation.
