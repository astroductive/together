How it works

Each round deals ten signs from the active vocabulary. For every target you first watch the reference — the animated motion for ASL or the real video clip for Egyptian ArSL — then perform it at your camera. The live recognition pipeline scores your attempt in real time: a match advances your streak, a miss lets you retry or reveal the reference again.

Attempts, hits, accuracy and streak are tracked on screen as you go, so progress is visible within a single session — and because the judge is the production recognition model, a sign that passes in Practice is a sign the live translator will understand.

Practice against the real thing

Most learning apps compare your attempt to a stored reference video. Practice runs your camera through the exact same landmark extraction and TFLite/CNN-GRU inference used in live conversation — so "correct" here means correct where it matters: the moment you sign to another person through Together.
