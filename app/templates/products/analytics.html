How it works

Every sign the recognition engine accepts — on the dashboard, in Practice, or during a SignLine meeting — is logged locally with its word, confidence score, language and timestamp. Analytics reads that log and renders your discoveries: which signs you use most, how recognition confidence trends over time, and how active your sessions are.

The log lives in your browser's local storage, capped at the most recent 2,000 detections. Nothing is uploaded; clearing your browser data clears the history. One click exports the raw log as CSV for a spreadsheet, or a formatted print view for a PDF report.
